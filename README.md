# Paraform

Work your Paraform roles, candidates and hiring from your AI assistant. Get answers from your Paraform account, in plain language.

Lets Claude work in your Paraform account. Ask in plain language and Claude answers from your real roles, candidates and pipeline, then acts for you when you ask.

For recruiters, Claude can find new roles that fit you or a specific candidate, check whether a candidate is eligible for a role, and pull a role's full details to draft outreach, screening questions and candidate write-ups. It can add and update candidates, prepare and submit them, recap past calls and their action items, and show your earnings, credits and what to work on today.

For hiring managers, Claude can show where every candidate stands across your roles and summarize interview feedback and recruiter notes for each one.

Claude acts as you. It sees only what your Paraform account can see and does only what your account allows. Before anything changes your account, Claude shows you the change and waits for your yes. Sign in with your Paraform account; there are no API keys to manage.

## Get started

Install the plugin, then sign in with your Paraform account when asked. Try:

- What should I focus on in Paraform today?
- Which roles fit my newest candidate?
- Which candidates are waiting on my review?
